Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ainkiwi Interactive ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in our website and mobile games. If you have any questions, contact us at contact@ainkiwi.com.

Information we collect

  • Device & usage data (e.g., device model, OS version, language, country, IP address, analytics events) to operate, secure, and improve our games.
  • Crash & performance data to diagnose issues and improve stability.
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Apple IDFA / Google Advertising ID) where applicable, to support ads and measure performance. You can reset or limit these in your device settings.
  • Contact information only if you voluntarily email us or submit a form (e.g., support requests). We use it solely to respond.

How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and improve our games and website.
  • Monitor performance, fix bugs, and combat fraud or abuse.
  • Personalize or measure advertising where permitted by law and platform rules.
  • Communicate with you about support inquiries.

Third‑party services

Our games may include SDKs or services from third parties to deliver analytics, ads, and crash reporting. These partners may process limited data under their own policies. Common examples include:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase (analytics/crash reporting)
  • Google AdMob or other ad networks (advertising)
  • Unity (game engine / analytics / ads)

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers. Availability may vary per title and platform.

Children's privacy

Our games are generally intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under the age required by local law to consent to data processing. Where we choose to publish a child‑directed experience, we implement measures consistent with applicable laws (such as COPPA or GDPR‑K) and platform requirements.

International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards to protect your data.

Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal data, and to object or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have a right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Opt‑outs & controls

  • Advertising IDs: reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Push notifications: manage permissions in your device settings.
  • Analytics/ads consent: where required, we ask for your consent in‑app; you can change your choice in the game's settings if available.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the latest version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@ainkiwi.com.
